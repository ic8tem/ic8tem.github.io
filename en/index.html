📡 YA-NEC – video course updated to 1.5.2

Lessons 0, 1, 13, 15, 22 and 27 re-recorded with 1.5.2, plus the new lesson 28: surveying a built antenna and the boom correction in reverse. Example: IC8TEM's 10-element Yagi on a 30 mm round boom, calculated as it is, resonates at 145.97 MHz; with Calculators › VHF › Boom correction in the sense «survey → electrical model for the NEC» it is back at 144.33 MHz, SWR 1.15, 15.7 dBi. 29 lessons in all, in order on YouTube.

YA-NEC 1.5.2

Yet Another NEC

NEC-2 antenna simulator for radio amateurs: wire antennas, dipoles and traps, HF beams, receiving antennas, VHF/UHF Yagis, stacks and G/T, optimizer, real terrain. Opens NEC2, MMANA, AutoEZ/MultiNEC and EZNEC files. 29-lesson video course.

YA-MS 1.8.22

Yet Another MS

Meteor scatter 50–432 MHz: forecasts and live efficiency of meteor showers on your path, which shower works right now, best headings (Hot A / Hot B), path geometry and losses, live meteor radar data, MS spots from PSKReporter, grid-square map, technical documents.

⬇️ Windows⬇️ Linux⬇️ macOS Apple Silicon⬇️ macOS IntelAll systems
Full descriptionAll filesGitHub

YAEC 1.9.5

Yet Another EME Calc

Plan your EME contacts: Moon position from your locator and common windows with the DX station, Doppler and frequency correction, degradation (DGRD), link budget for Yagis and dishes, sky and solar noise. «My horizon»: your real terrain profile from elevation data, with the Moon path and the exact times it rises and sets behind your obstacles. 37 languages.

⬇️ Windows⬇️ Linux⬇️ macOS Apple Silicon⬇️ macOS IntelAll systems

E-LAYER ANALYZER 0.5.3

VHF propagation via the E layer

Sporadic-E (also multi-hop and intercontinental), FAI, meteor scatter (with YA-MS built in) and radio aurora; live GIRO ionosondes and Es clouds over time. 35 languages, updates itself.

IP-TRX-CTRL 0.3.5

Remote control of your HF radio

Remote control of your HF radio over the internet: two-way AES-256 encrypted audio, CAT with Hamlib or TCI, PTT, CW keyer and decoder, digital modes on the client, access from a phone browser too. One program for both PCs: server (at the radio) and client (the operator).
