IC8TEM'S SOFTWARE FARM – Observe · Calculate · Share

Free ham radio software by Costantino Cerrotta IC8TEM (Capri, JN70CN): antennas, meteor scatter, EME, E-layer propagation and remote control of your radio. For Windows, macOS, Linux and Raspberry Pi, in 35-37 languages, with automatic updates.

Programs

All free. For other systems (Windows 7, ARM, Raspberry Pi, .deb, .rpm…) use the file finder for your system below.

YA-NEC

Yet Another NEC 1.7.0

NEC-2 antenna simulator for radio amateurs: wire antennas, dipoles and traps, HF beams, receiving antennas, VHF/UHF Yagis, stacks and G/T, optimizer, real terrain. Opens NEC2, MMANA, AutoEZ/MultiNEC and EZNEC files. 32-lesson video course.

YA-MS

Yet Another MS 1.8.22

Meteor scatter 50–432 MHz: forecasts and live efficiency of meteor showers on your path, which shower works right now, best headings (Hot A / Hot B), path geometry and losses, live meteor radar data, MS spots from PSKReporter, grid-square map, technical documents.

⬇️ Windows⬇️ Linux⬇️ macOS Apple Silicon⬇️ macOS IntelAll systems

YAEC

Yet Another EME Calc 1.9.5

Plan your EME contacts: Moon position from your locator and common windows with the DX station, Doppler and frequency correction, degradation (DGRD), link budget for Yagis and dishes, sky and solar noise. «My horizon»: your real terrain profile from elevation data, with the Moon path and the exact times it rises and sets behind your obstacles. 37 languages.

⬇️ Windows⬇️ Linux⬇️ macOS Apple Silicon⬇️ macOS IntelAll systems

E-LAYER ANALYZER

VHF propagation via the E layer 0.5.3

Sporadic-E (also multi-hop and intercontinental), FAI, meteor scatter (with YA-MS built in) and radio aurora; live GIRO ionosondes and Es clouds over time. 35 languages, updates itself.

⬇️ Windows⬇️ Linux⬇️ macOS Apple Silicon⬇️ macOS IntelAll systems (also experimental FreeBSD)

IP-TRX-CTRL

Remote control of your HF radio 0.3.7

Remote control of your HF radio over the internet: two-way AES-256 encrypted audio, CAT with Hamlib or TCI, PTT, CW keyer and decoder, digital modes on the client, access from a phone browser too. One program for both PCs: server (at the radio) and client (the operator).

⬇️ Windows⬇️ Linux⬇️ macOS Apple Silicon⬇️ macOS IntelAll systems
Full descriptionAll filesGitHub☕ Ko-fi

LIVETROPOVHF

Live VHF/UHF tropo 1.3.5

Live VHF/UHF tropospheric propagation from 144 MHz up: Hepburn and F5LEN forecast maps, live spots from DX clusters, RBN, PSKReporter and your own WSJT-X/JTDX/MSHV decodes, own forecast with link budget between two stations (terrain, diffraction, troposcatter, ducts, noise, digital modes). Windows 7 too.

📶 YA-NEC 1.7

YA-NEC 1.7, the NEC-2 antenna simulator, is out. New calculators from reading RadioRivista, RadioKit, QST and the ARRL Handbook: 2-3 element HF Yagi with tuning guide, interlaced dual-band Yagi, trap multiband vertical, inverted L / T for 160 m, half-sloper on the tower, broadband dipole, relay multiband dipole, linear loading, wire resonant on harmonics (EFLW), gamma match and stub matching with the Smith chart. 📻 IP-TRX-CTRL 0.3.7

IP-TRX-CTRL 0.3.7, remote control of your radio over the internet, is out. New: HamNoise neural noise reduction for voice and CW; VOX with a button next to PTT; notes between operator and listeners, also from the browser; listeners also hear whoever transmits (handy for nets); voice squelch; QSO recording to WAV; direct IPv6 connection with ports opened via PCP, which also gets past CGNAT; server without a window for a headless Raspberry Pi; WinKeyer emulation for N1MM, DXLog and QLog. Since 0.3.6: Opus audio, several listeners on one server, USB and MIDI CW key (VBand, Vail), QSO log to GridTracker, JTAlert and Log4OM with ADIF export for LoTW, networked Icom radios with no PC next to them. GNU AGPL 3.0 licence.
